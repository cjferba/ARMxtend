Citing ARMxtend

If you use ARMxtend as part of your workflow in a scientific publication, please consider citing the paper(s) behind the specific module(s) you used. For studies that apply these methods to real data see Applications.

To cite the software itself use the CITATION.cff file at the root of the repository (GitHub shows it under "Cite this repository"). There is no Zenodo DOI yet.

Methods implemented from the wider literature

Besides the papers above, some modules implement classic methods from other authors; please cite the originals when you use them:

  • Rules with absent items (ARM.negative_rules): Delgado, Ruiz, Sanchez & Serrano (2011, see above); restriction to items whose positive form is frequent: Han & Beheshti, as cited in refs. [18, 19] of that paper.
  • Exception rules (ARM.exception_rules): Suzuki, E. (1997). Autonomous discovery of reliable exception rules. KDD-97. Anomalous rules: Delgado, M., Ruiz, M.D., Sanchez, D. (2011). New approaches for discovering exception and anomalous rules. Int. J. Uncertainty, Fuzziness and Knowledge-Based Systems, 19(2), 361-399.
  • Gradual rules (ARM.gradual_rules): Hullermeier, E. (2002). Association rules for expressing gradual dependencies. PKDD 2002, LNCS 2431, 200-211.
  • Closed / maximal itemsets (FIM.condensed): Pasquier, N., Bastide, Y., Taouil, R., Lakhal, L. (1999). Discovering frequent closed itemsets for association rules. ICDT'99; Bayardo, R. (1998). Efficiently mining long patterns from databases. SIGMOD'98.
  • Redundant rules (ARM.redundancy): Bastide, Y., Taouil, R., Pasquier, N., Stumme, G., Lakhal, L. (2000). Mining minimal non-redundant association rules using frequent closed itemsets. CL 2000.
  • Interest measures (ARM.association_rules): Zhang, T. (2000), Association rules, PAKDD; Tan, P.-N., Kumar, V., Srivastava, J. (2004), Selecting the right objective measure for association analysis, Information Systems 29(4); Wu, T., Chen, Y., Han, J. (2010), Re-examination of interestingness measures in pattern mining: a unified framework, Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery 21(3).
  • Temporal (inter-transaction) rules (ARM.temporal_rules): Lu, H., Han, J., Feng, L. (1998). Stock movement prediction and N-dimensional inter-transaction association rules. ACM SIGMOD Workshop on Research Issues in Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery; Tung, A.K.H., Lu, H., Han, J., Feng, L. (2003). Efficient mining of intertransaction association rules. IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering 15(1), 43-56.
  • Symbolic time series (preprocessing.timeseries, method="sax"): Lin, J., Keogh, E., Wei, L., Lonardi, S. (2007). Experiencing SAX: a novel symbolic representation of time series. Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery 15, 107-144 (applied point-wise, without the PAA aggregation).
  • Sequential patterns (FIM.sequential): Pei, J., Han, J., Mortazavi-Asl, B., Wang, J., Pinto, H., Chen, Q., Dayal, U., Hsu, M.-C. (2004). Mining sequential patterns by pattern-growth: the PrefixSpan approach. IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering 16(11), 1424-1440.
  • Sequential rules (ARM.sequential_rules): Fournier-Viger, P., Wu, C.-W., Tseng, V.S., Cao, L., Nkambou, R. (2015). Mining partially-ordered sequential rules common to multiple sequences. IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering 27(8), 2203-2216 (definition of a valid rule; the search is not the RuleGrowth algorithm).
  • Multilevel / generalized rules (FIM.taxonomy, ARM.generalized_rules): Srikant, R., Agrawal, R. (1995). Mining generalized association rules. Proceedings of the 21st VLDB Conference, 407-419 (extended baskets, pruning of itemsets with an item and its ancestor, R-interesting rules); Han, J., Fu, Y. (1995). Discovery of multiple-level association rules from large databases. Proceedings of the 21st VLDB Conference, 420-431 (a support threshold per level; here an itemset takes the threshold of its most general item).
  • Quantitative rules (ARM.quantitative_rules): Srikant, R., Agrawal, R. (1996). Mining quantitative association rules in large relational tables. Proceedings of the 1996 ACM SIGMOD International Conference on Management of Data, 1-12 (equal-frequency base intervals merged into ranges up to a maximum support, partial completeness, R-interesting rules); the implementation reduces the ranges to the hierarchy of ARM.generalized_rules.
  • Top-k rules (FIM.topk, ARM.topk_rules): Fournier-Viger, P., Tseng, V.S. (2012). Mining top-k association rules. Proceedings of the 25th Canadian Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 61-73 (the problem; the best-first search over itemsets used here is the library's own, not their algorithm).
  • Rare itemsets and rules (FIM.rare, ARM.rare_rules): Szathmary, L., Napoli, A., Valtchev, P. (2007). Towards rare itemset mining. Proceedings of the 19th IEEE International Conference on Tools with Artificial Intelligence, 305-312 (minimal rare itemsets).
  • High-utility itemsets and rules (FIM.utility, ARM.utility_rules): Yao, H., Hamilton, H.J., Butz, C.J. (2004). A foundational approach to mining itemset utilities from databases. Proceedings of the 4th SIAM International Conference on Data Mining, 482-486; Liu, Y., Liao, W., Choudhary, A. (2005). A two-phase algorithm for fast discovery of high utility itemsets. PAKDD 2005, 689-695 (transaction-weighted utility).
  • Fuzzy sequential patterns and rules (FIM.fuzzy_sequential, ARM.fuzzy_sequential_rules): inspired by Fiot, C., Laurent, A., Teisseire, M. (2007). From crispness to fuzziness: three algorithms for soft sequential pattern mining. IEEE Transactions on Fuzzy Systems 15(6), 1263-1277; the definitions (best embedding, t-norm, mean over the sequences) are the library's own. Triangular norms: Klement, E.P., Mesiar, R., Pap, E. (2000), Triangular Norms, Kluwer.
  • Rule drift (ARM.rule_drift): the batch counterpart of the trends kept incrementally by FIMoTS (Fernandez-Basso et al., 2019, see above).
  • Statistical significance of rules (ARM.significance): Fisher's exact test; Holm, S. (1979), A simple sequentially rejective multiple test procedure, Scandinavian Journal of Statistics 6, 65-70; Benjamini, Y., Hochberg, Y. (1995), Controlling the false discovery rate, J. Royal Statistical Society B 57, 289-300; Benjamini, Y., Yekutieli, D. (2001), Annals of Statistics 29, 1165-1188; Westfall, P.H., Young, S.S. (1993), Resampling-based multiple testing; Webb, G.I. (2007), Discovering significant patterns, Machine Learning 68, 1-33; Wilson, E.B. (1927), JASA 22, 209-212.
  • Rules in action (ARM.anomaly, ARM.prediction, ARM.classification): Liu, B., Hsu, W., Ma, Y. (1998), Integrating classification and association rule mining, Proc. KDD-98, 80-86 (CBA); Liu, B., Ma, Y., Wong, C.-K. (2000), Improving an association rule based classifier, PKDD 2000; Bayardo, R.J., Agrawal, R., Gunopulos, D. (2000), Constraint-based rule mining in large, dense databases, Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery 4, 217-240 (improvement of a rule, prune_unproductive_rules).
